YayPricing — dynamic pricing & automated discounts for WooCommerce

  • Works with any theme & page builder
  • Tested with Woo 10.5 and WordPress 7.1

YayPricing puts every kind of discount in one rule builder — BOGO deals, bulk and tiered pricing, cart discounts, scheduled flash sales and role-based prices — and applies them automatically at checkout.
